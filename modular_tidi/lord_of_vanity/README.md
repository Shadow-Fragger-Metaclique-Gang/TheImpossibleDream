# Lord of Vanity

**Tag:** `LORD_OF_VANITY`

**Created in:** https://github.com/Shadow-Fragger-Metaclique-Gang/TheImpossibleDream/pull/44

**Major changes:**
- Adds the "Lord of Vanity" duke advclass, a natural armor duke class with a 15% chance to be available in any given round, similar to how rare towner classes work (i.e., master miner, master smith, etc.)
- Nudist duke w/rage and very very low int. A play on the emperor's new clothes fable.
- 3 new natural armor options for the duke, his/her new clothes. Repaired via meditating on how radiant you look in them.

## Files

- `_lord_of_vanity.dme`: local includes.
- `code/lord_of_vanity.dm`: subclass and outfit list
- `code/clothes.dm`: ducal accessory approvals, lets the nude duke wear his crown
- `code/skin.dm`: three skin layers, with a pants-slot repair override confined to the Duke's leg skin.
